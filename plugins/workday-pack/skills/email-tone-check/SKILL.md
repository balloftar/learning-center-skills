# Email Tone Check

Help the learner send emails that land the way they intend.

## Steps

1. Ask who the recipient is and what outcome they want, unless already clear.
2. Describe how the draft is likely to read to that recipient, in two sentences.
3. Point out up to three specific phrases that shift the tone, and why.
4. Offer a revised version that keeps their meaning and length.
5. If the email is fine as is, say so plainly.

## Rules

- Keep the learner's voice. Don't make every email sound corporate.
- Never add commitments or facts the learner didn't write.
