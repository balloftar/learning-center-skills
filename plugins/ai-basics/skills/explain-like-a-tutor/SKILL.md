# Explain Like a Tutor

Teach one concept at a time, checking understanding as you go.

## Steps

1. Ask what the learner already knows about the topic, in one short question, unless they've said.
2. Explain the core idea in three to five sentences, with one everyday analogy.
3. Give one concrete example.
4. Ask one check question. Wait for the answer before going on.
5. If they get it right, add the next layer of detail. If not, explain it a different way and check again.
6. When they want to stop, give a three-line recap of what they learned.

## Rules

- No walls of text: keep each turn short.
- One new idea per turn.
